# Committees and Appointments
## Overview
The "Committees and Appointments" page allows you to manage the committees and appointments.
You can view and edit the members of each committee or appointment position, as well as their significant dates.

This feature is implemented on both austinquakers.org and scmy.org with only minor differences such as naming and menu position:<br>
SCYM.ORG: Directory > Appointments & Committees<br>
AustinQuakers.org: Community > Directory > Committees & Appointments

The committee/appointment information stored in the database is used to provide source data for several other features,
such as the "Committee pages" and "Distribution lists".  For instance, "Committee pages" display committee description and
a list of current members that are derived from the data you enter on "Committees & Appointments".
"Distribution lists" get email addresses from this source as well.

Personal information such as email addresses and phone numbers are stored in the "Meeting Directory" or "Yearly Meeting Directory".
This means that when changes are made, such as a change to the mail address of a committee member, the change will be
reflected in the "Committee pages" and "Distribution lists" as well a mailing lists.

An "appointment" works just like a committee with only one member. There is no real functional difference.

It will help to have a sense of how the information is organized. Normally we tend to think of a committee list as simply
a list of members.  Here we associate comittees and members with a "term of service" indicating when they joined, when
their service ended and the status of their nomination.  This allows us to list not only current members, but previous
appointments and pending nominations and first-readings.  This may make more sense when you get a look at how these pages operate.

## Viewing and Making Changes

Here is a simple example of how to view make changes to committees and appointments.  These screenshots are from SCMY.org,
but the same feature in austinquakes.org is very similar. This demonstration is from the point-of-view of someone who
has permission to make changes. Users who do not have permission will see much the same except that there will be no buttons for
making and saving changes and no data input forms.  To view the information you need to be signed-in to the website.

![committees-main.jpg](img/committees-main.jpg)

Select a Committee of appointment position from the drop-down list.  In this example we will select 'Yearly Meeting Clerk'.

![select-clerk.jpg](img/select-clerk.jpg)

![committees-clerk-screen.jpg](img/committees-clerk-screen.jpg)

Since the Clerk is a single member postion, only one person is listed.  If you select a committee, such as 'Technology Committee', you will see a list of all the members.

![committees-tech-screen.jpg](img/committees-tech-screen.jpg)

In 2026 Kevin King was appointed and approved as Yearly Meeting Clerk, replacing the previous Clerk, Richard Corl.
This is how we make that change in the system. First we will click 'Add new member' and add Kevin as clerk.

![committees-new-member.jpg](img/committees-new-member.jpg)

Search and select 'Kevin King'.

![committees-select-member-1.jpg](img/committees-select-member-1.jpg)

The member input accepts these fields:
- Status (Nominated, First Reading, Approved, Withdrawn, )
- Start of Service - the year the appointment begins.
- End of Service - the end date of the term of service.
- Relieved - the actual date the person left the position. It is typically different from "End of Service", because plans may change.

Click 'Save Changes' when ready.

![committees-new-appointment.jpg](img/committees-new-appointment.jpg)

Now both Kevin King and Richard Corl are listed as Clerk, so we need to remove Richards name as a current appointee.
First click on Richard Corl's name.  And in the member form enter the date that he was relieved from the clerkship,
and click 'Save Changes'.

![committees-relieved-date.jpg](img/committees-relieved-date.jpg)

Now only Kevin King is listed as the current clerk and our task is complete.

If you want to see a list of previous appointments, click on the drop down list. And select 'Current and Former appointees'.

![committees-select-former.jpg](img/committees-select-former.jpg)

Now you can see a list of previous appointments.

![committees-former-clerks.jpg](img/committees-former-clerks.jpg)
